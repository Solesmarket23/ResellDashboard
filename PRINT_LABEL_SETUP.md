# Printing Shipping Labels: iPhone → Mac → DYMO 4XL

Print from the app on your phone; the job goes to your Mac, which prints on the DYMO 4XL (USB).

---

## 1. Mac: Share the printer

1. Connect the DYMO 4XL to your Mac via USB.
2. Open **System Settings** (or System Preferences) → **Printers & Scanners**.
3. Select the DYMO 4XL.
4. Turn **on** **“Share this printer on the network”**.

Your Mac must be on and awake when you print from the phone.

---

## 2. Mac: Set 4×6 as default (optional but recommended)

1. In **Printers & Scanners**, select the DYMO 4XL.
2. Click **Options and Supplies** (or **Options**).
3. Set the default **Paper Size** to **4 × 6 in** (or your 4×6 preset).

Jobs sent from the iPhone will use this when they hit the Mac.

---

## 3. iPhone: Same Wi‑Fi as the Mac

Your iPhone and Mac must be on the **same Wi‑Fi network** so the phone can see the shared printer.

---

## 4. Print from the app

1. In the app, go to **Ship** → **Print shipping label**.
2. Enter the order number and tap **Print shipping label**.
3. When the print sheet appears, tap **Printer** and choose **your DYMO** (or the printer listed under your Mac’s name).
4. Tap **Print**.

The job is sent to your Mac; the Mac sends it to the DYMO using its settings (including 4×6 if you set it).

---

## 5. If the label prints rotated 90°

Some label printers (including the 4XL) feed in a way that makes the first print look rotated.

- In the app, turn **on** **“Landscape orientation”** (below the Thermal label toggle).
- Print again.

One of **Portrait** (default) or **Landscape** will match your printer. Leave that option set for future labels.

---

## Summary

| Step | Where | What to do |
|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Mac | Share the DYMO printer on the network |
| 2 | Mac | Set default paper size to 4×6 for that printer |
| 3 | Both | iPhone and Mac on same Wi‑Fi |
| 4 | iPhone | Print → choose the shared DYMO → Print |
| 5 | iPhone | If output is rotated, use “Landscape orientation” and print again |

No Terminal or extra software needed. The app uses the system print sheet; you just pick the shared printer.
